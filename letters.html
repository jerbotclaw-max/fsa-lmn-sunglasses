LMN Form Letters — UV-Filter & Tinted Eyewear

Five defensible letter-of-medical-necessity templates for FSA/HSA reimbursement · panel-reviewed against real administrator criteria · every clinical claim cited · Aug 29, 2026
Read first — how to use these honestly. These are templates for a licensed provider (MD/DO, OD, NP) to complete after an actual evaluation. The letter is only defensible if the clinical statements in it are true for the patient being certified. Using a template does not make an item eligible — the administrator still has final discretion, and the provider is signing a document they own. Blanket use without an exam is how LMNs get denied and providers get burned.

What makes an LMN survive review

From administrator review criteria (see panel notes below): (1) a specific, billable diagnosis — not "sun protection"; (2) explicit tie from diagnosis → why UV/tint attenuation treats it; (3) lens specs (UV400, tint category/FL-41 wavelength, prescription detail); (4) duration; (5) provider NPI/license, signature, date; (6) an exam finding or documented history backing the diagnosis. Weaker letters name a risk factor alone ("blue eyes", "age") without a symptomatic or exam finding — reviewers flag those as dual-use/comfort claims.
